What Burnout Looks Like

Burnout builds quietly before you realize how depleted you are.

Many driven people push through warning signs for months until focus drops, patience runs thin, and everyday tasks feel heavy.

Emotional numbness or irritability

Snapping over small things, feeling detached from tasks you used to care about, or feeling flat even on days off.

Cognitive overload and slow focus

Re-reading simple messages, struggling to prioritize, or feeling mentally saturated before mid afternoon.

Always on, never truly off

Checking messages late, thinking about unfinished tasks in bed, and treating downtime like wasted time.
