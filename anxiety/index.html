Why It Feels So Heavy

Anxiety rarely stays limited to a few anxious thoughts.

Over time, it spills into your body, your sleep, and the everyday choices you make just to feel safe.

Physical tension that will not settle

Racing heart, tight chest, dizziness, nausea, shaky hands, or waking up already feeling wired even when nothing urgent is happening.

Overthinking every possible outcome

Replaying conversations, scanning for danger, seeking constant reassurance, or lying awake with thoughts that refuse to slow down.

Quietly shrinking your routine

Canceling plans, putting off calls, or avoiding normal places because you do not trust how your body or mind will react.

Questions & Answers

Common things people wonder before starting.

Are the therapists qualified and licensed?

Yes. You are matched with a vetted, licensed mental health professional experienced in cognitive behavioral therapy for anxiety, panic, and stress.

Do I have to do video calls right away?

No. You can choose live video, voice only, or text messaging depending on what feels most comfortable for you right now.

What if my anxiety is mostly physical sensations?

That is very common. Cognitive behavioral therapy specifically addresses how your nervous system reacts to bodily sensations and helps break the fear cycle.
