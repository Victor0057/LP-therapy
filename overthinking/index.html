Breaking The Cycle

Thinking harder rarely solves a habit of overthinking.

Most overthinkers do not lack intelligence or insight. They get stuck trying to think their way to 100% certainty.

The overthinking trap

  • Chasing certainty by running dozens of what if scenarios
  • Re-reading messages or replaying talks looking for hidden meaning
  • Delaying choices until the risk of being wrong feels smaller
  • Feeling mentally exhausted even without physical exertion

What changes with guided support

  • Catching thought spirals earlier before they snowball
  • Separating useful planning from repetitive mental rumination
  • Taking clean, grounded action without needing perfection first
  • Having a licensed therapist help you test new responses weekly

You can choose live sessions by video or voice, or work through text messaging depending on what feels comfortable.
